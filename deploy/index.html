Questions from grown-ups

Is any real money involved?

No. Coins, stars and Money City dollars are all pretend. Money City uses real market prices so the practice feels real, but nothing is ever bought or sold with real money.

What ages is it for?

Junior Quest is for ages 6–11 and every card can be read aloud. Pro Quest is for teens and follows the Missouri high-school Personal Finance course. Each player picks a track and can switch any time.

What devices does it work on?

Anything with a modern web browser: phones, tablets, Chromebooks, Macs and PCs. You can also add it to your home screen like an app. Nothing to install.

How long does a lesson take?

About five minutes. A daily goal is roughly one or two lessons, and the game suggests a break after 15 minutes of play.

Can teachers assign specific units?

Yes. In Pro Quest the first lesson of every world is open, so a class can start on any unit, such as credit or investing, on day one.
