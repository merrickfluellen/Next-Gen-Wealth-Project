Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Applies to Money Quest by InvestEd Pro at nextgenwealthproject.netlify.app (the home page, the app, and its server functions).

The short version

  • To make an account we ask your age, a first name or nickname, an email address, a password and an access code. We save only your age group (13 to 17, or 18 and older), never your exact age. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Children under 13 cannot sign themselves up. If someone picks "12 or younger", sign-up stops and nothing is sent to us.
  • We save your learning and game progress so it follows you to other devices.
  • We do not sell personal information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • The optional "Find my home" game feature uses a street address once to estimate a value. The address is not saved. We ask kids to use it only with a parent. See section 5.
  • The "Delete my data" button on the Profile page permanently deletes the account and its saved data from our servers. Parents can also ask us to review or delete their child's information. See section 8 and section 13.

1. Who we are

Money Quest by InvestEd Pro ("Money Quest", "we", "us") is a money-education game for kids and teens, made and run by Merrick Fluellen. It has two tracks: Junior Quest (designed for ages 6–11) and Pro Quest (for teens). Everything in the app is a simulation for learning. No real money is invested and no real property is bought or sold.

3. How we use information

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To save and sync your progress, notes and game state.
  • To record the age group and agreement given at sign-up.
  • To send password-reset codes by email (the only emails we send).
  • To check access codes and keep track of which codes have been used.
  • To estimate pretend home values and show maps in the Money City game.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.

We do not use personal information for advertising, we do not build marketing profiles, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

4. What stays on your device

The app saves information in your browser's local storage so it works quickly. This includes your sign-in token and the name and email shown in the app, your progress and game state, lesson notes, sound and graphics settings, and a list of up to 1,000 recent in-app actions with their times (for example "finished a lesson"; it contains no names, emails or addresses and is never sent to us). Some temporary items (such as the owner's admin sign-in) use session storage, which clears when the tab is closed.

If someone picks "12 or younger" at sign-up, the browser remembers that so sign-up stays blocked on that device. This note never leaves the browser.

Signing out removes your progress, notes, game saves and sign-in from that browser (they stay saved in your account). "Delete my data" on the Profile page deletes your account and everything saved with it from our servers, and also removes it from that browser. Sound and graphics settings stay on the device.

5. "Find my home" and maps

Money City has an optional Pro Quest feature called "Find my home" that estimates what a real home might be worth.

  • When you use it, the address you type is sent to our server (inside the request, not in the web address), which sends it to the U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder to find the location and neighborhood, and, if enabled, to RentCast for a value estimate. To avoid repeat lookups, our server keeps results in temporary memory for up to 1 hour. It does not log the address or write it to our database, and it tells your browser not to keep a copy.
  • The street address, ZIP code and exact location are not saved, in your browser or in our database. The map moves to a point about 1 km (0.01°) from the home, not to the house itself. If you "buy" that home in the game, it is saved with your game progress as "My home", with its estimated value, the city and state, and that rounded map position. Homes saved by older versions of the app are cleaned the same way the next time the account opens the app.
  • Map searches for cities, ZIP codes or addresses are sent by our server to OpenStreetMap Nominatim and the Census Geocoder. A searched street address also only moves the map near the address, not onto it. Search results are kept in temporary server memory for up to 20 minutes and are not logged.
  • When the map is open, your browser loads map pictures from a map tile provider and asks Overpass (OpenStreetMap) servers for building data for the area on screen. These services see your IP address and the area you are viewing.

Kids: please only use "Find my home" with a parent or guardian. Parents: the feature does not save your address, and you can delete any game data at any time (see section 9).

8. Children's privacy (COPPA notice for parents)

Money Quest is built for young learners, including children under 13 (Junior Quest), so we follow the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) principles below. This section is our notice to parents.

What we collect from children and why

The same information described in section 2: a name, an email address and password to make an account, the age group and agreement record, progress and game data, any lesson notes the child types, and (only if used) a home address for "Find my home", which is not saved. We use it only to run the learning game, as described in section 3. We do not require a child to give more information than is reasonably needed to take part, and children cannot post information publicly or chat with other users in Money Quest.

Sign-up and parental consent

Sign-up asks for the user's age first. If the user is 12 or younger, sign-up stops before any name, email or password is asked for, nothing is sent to us, and the child is asked to have a parent or teacher set up Money Quest. Children under 13 cannot create their own account.

A parent, guardian or teacher may create an account with their own details and age, and let a child play on it with them. We do not collect a separate record from or about the child in that case; the account is the adult's, and the adult agrees to the Terms and this policy for the child. A school may do this on behalf of parents when Money Quest is used only for classroom learning.

Users aged 13 to 17 must tick a box confirming they read the Terms and this policy with a parent or guardian and that they both agree. We save their age group (13 to 17), the version of the Terms and policy they agreed to, and the date and time.

If we learn that we collected personal information from a child under 13 without a parent's consent, we will delete it.

How parents can review or delete data

A parent or guardian can, at any time:

  • Delete the account and everything saved with it right away, using "Delete my data" on the Profile page while signed in, or ask us to do it.
  • Review the personal information we hold about their child.
  • Correct it, or ask us to correct it.
  • Revoke consent and stop any further collection or use of their child's information. This closes the account, because the game cannot save progress without it.

To make a request, contact us as described in section 13. Tell us the account email. To protect the child, we will take reasonable steps to confirm that you are the parent or guardian before we share or change anything. We will respond within 30 days, usually much sooner. When an account is deleted, the access-code use record keeps only a one-way hash of the code (so the code cannot be used again) and the email is removed from it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete: "Delete my data" on the Profile page permanently deletes your account, progress, notes, game saves and agreement record from our database, and removes them from that browser. It cannot be undone, and the access code will not work again.
  • Access or correct: anyone (not only parents) can ask us for a copy of their information or to fix it. Contact us (section 13).
  • Local data: signing out removes your information from that browser (it stays in your account). You can also clear it from your browser's settings.
  • Optional features: you do not have to use "Find my home", lesson notes or the feedback form.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Economic Area), you may have additional rights. Contact us and we will honor them as the law requires.

10. How long we keep information

  • Account, age group, agreement record, progress and notes: while the account exists. "Delete my data" deletes them from our database immediately. Requests sent to us are completed within 30 days. Copies in our database provider's routine backups expire on the provider's own schedule.
  • Access-code use records: kept to prevent a code from being reused. When the account is deleted the email is removed; the code hash is kept so the code cannot be used again.
  • Password-reset requests: the 6-digit code expires after 15 minutes and is never stored. The request record (a one-way version of the email, the time and the number of tries) is deleted within 2 days.
  • Abuse counters (labeled with an IP address, an IPv6 network or an email): deleted automatically within 2 days.
  • Sign-in sessions: a sign-in lasts up to 30 days and is renewed while you keep using the app. Changing your password or deleting the account ends every sign-in at once.
  • "Find my home" lookups and map searches: kept only in temporary server memory, for up to 1 hour. Buying the home in the game saves it without its address (see section 5).
  • Server and hosting logs: kept by Netlify for a limited time under its own policies.
  • Browser storage: stays on your device until you sign out, use "Delete my data", or clear it.

We do not keep children's personal information longer than needed to provide the service.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, including:

  • Encrypted connections: the whole site is served only over HTTPS, and HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) tells browsers never to connect without encryption.
  • Server-side accounts: sign-up and sign-in are checked by our server, which issues a sign-in token signed with HMAC-SHA256. The web page never reads or writes our database directly. The database is locked so that only our server can reach it, and the server only returns or changes the data of the account the token belongs to.
  • Hashed passwords and codes: passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, never in plain text. New and changed passwords use salted scrypt, and older accounts' hashes are upgraded when they sign in. New access codes and password-reset records are stored only as keyed one-way hashes.
  • Encrypted reset tokens: password-reset tokens are encrypted and authenticated with AES-256-GCM and expire after 15 minutes. A code can be guessed at most 5 times.
  • Abuse limits: sign-in, sign-up, password-reset, data-saving and map requests are rate-limited by counters shared by all of our servers, and a request filter blocks common attack patterns before they reach our code.
  • No outside code: every script the app runs comes from our own site, and our Content Security Policy tells browsers to refuse scripts from anywhere else.
  • Secrets on the server: database keys, signing keys and admin credentials are kept in server settings, not in the web page.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a security incident that affects your information, we will notify affected users (and parents, for children's accounts) as the law requires. To report a security problem, see the SECURITY policy on our GitHub repository.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top. If a change affects how we collect or use children's information in a significant way, we will notify parents and ask for consent again where COPPA requires it before the change applies to their child.
