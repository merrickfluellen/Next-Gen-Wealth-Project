1. Who we are

Money Quest by InvestEd Pro ("Money Quest", "we", "us") is a money-education game for kids and teens, made and run by Merrick Fluellen. It has two tracks: Junior Quest (designed for ages 6–11) and Pro Quest (for teens). Everything in the app is a simulation for learning. No real money is invested and no real property is bought or sold.

3. How we use information

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To save and sync your progress, notes and game state.
  • To send password-reset codes by email (the only emails we send).
  • To check access codes and keep track of which codes have been used.
  • To estimate pretend home values and show maps in the Money City game.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.

We do not use personal information for advertising, we do not build marketing profiles, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

4. What stays on your device

The app saves information in your browser's local storage so it works quickly and offline. This includes your signed-in session (name and email), your progress and game state, lesson notes, sound and graphics settings, and a short history of in-app activity used for troubleshooting. Some temporary items (such as sign-in attempt limits) use session storage and clear when you close the tab.

Information in local storage stays on that device until it is cleared. On a shared or school computer, sign out and clear the site's data (in your browser settings, delete site data for nextgenwealthproject.netlify.app) when you finish.

5. "Find my home" and maps

Money City has an optional Pro Quest feature called "Find my home" that estimates what a real home might be worth.

  • When you use it, the address you type is sent to our server, which sends it to the U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder to find the location and neighborhood, and, if enabled, to RentCast for a value estimate. Our server keeps results in temporary memory for up to about an hour to avoid repeat lookups and does not log or write the address to our database.
  • If you only look up the home, it is not saved. If you choose to "buy" that home in the game, the property, including its street address and map coordinates, is saved with your game progress in your browser and in our database.
  • Map searches for cities, ZIP codes or addresses go through our server to OpenStreetMap Nominatim and the Census Geocoder. When the map is open, your browser loads map pictures from a map tile provider and asks Overpass (OpenStreetMap) servers for building data for the area on screen. These services see your IP address and the area you are viewing.

Kids: please only use "Find my home" with a parent or guardian. Parents: you can ask us to delete a saved home address at any time (see section 13). We are working on changing this feature so it never saves an exact address.

6. Who we share information with

We share personal information only with the service providers that run Money Quest for us (listed below), only as needed to provide the service, and when the law requires it (for example, a valid court order) or to protect the safety of our users. If Money Quest were ever transferred to a new owner, this policy would continue to apply to information collected under it, and we would notify users first.

The site owner can see the list of accounts (name, email, account type and sign-up date) to manage access codes and support.

8. Children's privacy (COPPA notice for parents)

Money Quest is built for young learners, including children under 13, so we follow the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) principles below. This section is our notice to parents.

What we collect from children and why

The same information described in section 2: a name, an email address and password to make an account, progress and game data, any lesson notes the child types, and (only if used) a home address for "Find my home". We use it only to run the learning game for the child, as described in section 3. We do not require a child to give more information than is reasonably needed to take part, and children cannot post information publicly or chat with other users in Money Quest.

Parental consent

A child under 13 should not create an account or use Money Quest without a parent's or guardian's permission. Accounts are created with a purchased access code, and we expect a parent, guardian or teacher to set up and manage accounts for children under 13. A school may consent on behalf of parents when Money Quest is used only for classroom learning.

Our sign-up does not yet ask for age. We are adding an age check and a parental-consent step. If we learn that we collected personal information from a child under 13 without a parent's consent, we will delete it.

Parents' rights

A parent or guardian of a child under 13 can, at any time:

  • Review the personal information we hold about their child.
  • Correct it, or ask us to correct it.
  • Delete it, including the account, progress, notes and any saved home address.
  • Revoke consent and stop any further collection or use of their child's information. This closes the child's account, because the game cannot save progress without it.

To make a request, contact us as described in section 13. Tell us the child's account email. To protect the child, we will take reasonable steps to confirm that you are the parent or guardian before we share or change anything. We will respond within 30 days, usually much sooner. We will also remove the child's information from the access-code records we keep in Netlify Forms.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct or delete: anyone (not only parents) can ask us for a copy of their information, to fix it, or to delete their account and data. Contact us (section 13).
  • Local data: you can clear the information saved in your browser at any time from your browser's settings.
  • Optional features: you do not have to use "Find my home", lesson notes or the feedback form.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Economic Area), you may have additional rights. Contact us and we will honor them as the law requires.

10. How long we keep information

  • Account, progress and notes: while the account exists. When an account is deleted, we delete its data from our database within 30 days. Copies in a hosting provider's routine backups may take up to an additional 30 days to expire.
  • Access-code use records (Netlify Forms): kept to prevent a code from being reused and deleted on request.
  • Password-reset codes: expire after 15 minutes and are not stored in our database.
  • Sign-in sessions: expire after 30 days of not being renewed.
  • "Find my home" lookups: kept only in temporary server memory (up to about an hour), unless you save the home by buying it in the game.
  • Server and hosting logs: kept by Netlify for a limited time under its own policies.
  • Browser storage: stays on your device until you clear it.

We do not keep children's personal information longer than needed to provide the service.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, including:

  • Encrypted connections: the whole site is served only over HTTPS, and HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) tells browsers never to connect without encryption.
  • Hashed passwords: passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, never in plain text. We use salted scrypt hashing for new and updated passwords and upgrade older accounts' hashes when they sign in.
  • Encrypted reset tokens: password-reset verification tokens are encrypted and authenticated with AES-256-GCM and expire after 15 minutes.
  • Abuse limits: sign-in, sign-up and password-reset requests are rate-limited.
  • Secrets on the server: service keys and admin credentials are kept in server settings, not in the web page.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a security incident that affects your information, we will notify affected users (and parents, for children's accounts) as the law requires. To report a security problem, see the SECURITY policy on our GitHub repository.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top. If a change affects how we collect or use children's information in a significant way, we will notify parents and ask for consent again where COPPA requires it before the change applies to their child.
